## 2. TopstepX trading journal
Blog: `/blog/topstepx-trading-journal-track-topstep-combine` — keyword: *TopstepX trading journal*

### Thread de X
1. Passing the Topstep Combine isn't only about the profit target. Two numbers end most attempts: your Max Loss Limit and your best day.
2. Topstep's help center lists a 55% consistency target for the Combine: your best day must stay at or below 55% of your profit target. $50K account: under $1,650.
3. Example: you're at $2,000 of $3,000, then take a $1,900 day. That day alone now needs ~$3,455 total to fit the rule. A great day became a problem.
4. Log daily: distance to MLL, best day as % of target, trade count, plan followed yes/no.
5. Check rules at help.topstep.com before you start; they change. Full guide: [link]

## 4. Apex consistency rule 2026
Blog: `/blog/apex-consistency-rule-explained-2026` — keyword: *apex consistency rule*

### Thread de X
1. Apex's consistency rule trips traders at payout time. Per Apex's help center, on Performance Accounts your best day must be under 50% of net profit since your last approved payout.
2. Formula: best day ÷ net profit. $1,500 best day needs $3,000 net profit. At $2,600 you're at 57.7%: payout locked.
3. Losing days count. They shrink the denominator.
4. Legacy accounts bought before the March 2026 update may still follow a 30% rule. Check your purchase date.
5. Track the ratio daily, not on payout day. Full guide with table: [link]

## 6. Daily loss limit routine
Blog: `/blog/daily-loss-limit-routine-protect-funded-account` — keyword: *daily loss limit routine*

### Thread de X
1. Your firm's loss limit is the last line of defense. Your own daily limit should trigger first.
2. Take your real buffer (equity to the closing level). Use ~50% as your personal stop-for-the-day. $2,000 buffer = $1,000 limit, ~$250 max per trade.
3. Pre-session: write your limit, max trades, allowed setups, one-word emotional state.
4. In-session: two losses in a row = fixed cooling-off. Hit your limit = close the platform.
5. Post-session: log trades, mark planned vs impulsive, update tomorrow's limit. Full routine: [link]

**Hook alternativo:** Decide your stop-for-the-day before the open, not after the third loss.

### Caption LinkedIn/Instagram
A bad day stays small when the decision was made before the emotion arrived. Our routine: set a personal limit below the firm's, define stop rules, and review in a journal. Note: not every firm has a daily loss limit (TopstepX accounts created or reset after Aug 25, 2024 don't), but a personal limit still protects your drawdown. #riskmanagement #fundedtrader #propfirm #futurestrading #zentrade
